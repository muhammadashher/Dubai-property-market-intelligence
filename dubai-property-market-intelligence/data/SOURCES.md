# Data sources

All raw files in `data/raw/` are real, public, aggregated Dubai Land Department (DLD) open data.

| File(s) | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `market_*.csv`, `community_*.csv`, `communities.csv`, `rents_by_community.csv`, `yields_by_community.csv`, `areas.csv`, `developers.csv`, `projects.csv` | Dubai Data (Continental Club Property), "Dubai Real Estate — DLD open data, cleaned and aggregated", data through 2026-09-24. https://github.com/datadubai/dubai-real-estate-dld | CC BY 4.0 |
| `price_index_monthly.csv`, `rent_index_monthly.csv` | dataHabibi L.L.C-FZ, "Dubai residential price and rent index". https://github.com/dataHabibi/dubai-price-index | CC BY 4.0 |

Source data © Dubai Land Department, published as open data. These aggregates are not official DLD products.
Figures are medians; see the publishers' methodology pages for outlier trimming and minimum sample rules.
